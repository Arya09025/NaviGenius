NaviGenius data declaration

NaviGenius is designed to keep your personal information on your device for this prototype.

We do not sell your name, phone number, preferences or trip history to advertisers.
Your profile, preferences and trip history are stored locally in your browser for the prototype.
When you request a route, the places you search for may be sent to the map or routing service used to calculate the result.
NaviGenius does not intentionally retain a separate personal location history on its own server in this prototype.
You can clear the saved profile and trip history from your browser storage at any time.
